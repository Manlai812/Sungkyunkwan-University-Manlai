03 — The school

Sungkyunkwan University

Founded in 1398 as Korea's royal Confucian academy, SKKU is the oldest university in Korea — now a modern research university run in close partnership with Samsung, split across a Seoul campus and a Suwon campus.

Its founding ideology, 수기치인 (Su-Gi-Chi-In) — "perfect yourself, then lead others" — still shapes the school's culture today: rigorous, research-driven, and increasingly international, with close industry ties through Samsung.

What you can actually study here

Korea's first fully English-taught Global MBA, alongside a business school taught largely in English — built for students who aren't arriving fluent in Korean.

Software, mobile communications, energy engineering, and nanotechnology tracks co-developed with Samsung, based mainly on the Suwon Natural Sciences campus.

One of Korea's strongest medical programs, affiliated with Samsung Medical Center.

Eighteen schools and three colleges at the bachelor's level on the Seoul campus, rooted in SKKU's Confucian academic tradition.
